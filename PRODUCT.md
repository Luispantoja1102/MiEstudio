# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
SvelteKit 2 / Svelte 5 SPA (adapter-static) with plain CSS tokens in `Frontend/src/app.css`; Go API. Existing codebase: keep the stack.

## Users
Whole team of small and mid-size beauty businesses in Mexico (barberías, estéticas, spas, uñas), on a mix of phones, tablets and desktops:
reception and cashier at the counter (standing, in a hurry, interrupted by clients), stylists checking their day and commissions between clients,
and the owner reviewing sales and settings. A second audience is the end client, who books and manages an appointment from a phone, with no account.
The platform owner (a separate role) approves businesses and manages plans in a distinct panel.

## Product Purpose
Multi-business SaaS for appointment booking plus point of sale: agenda, online booking with deposits, cash register and Mercado Pago terminal,
inventory, commissions, packages, waitlist, reminders (email/WhatsApp), client messaging and privacy tooling. Success: a non-technical owner runs the
whole day from one place and clients stop no-showing.

## Positioning
Built for Mexico and for small and mid-size businesses: MXN pricing, Mercado Pago, WhatsApp, LFPDPPP privacy, simple enough to learn without training;
all-in-one (agenda + cash + inventory + deposits) instead of separate tools.

## Brand commitments
Tone confirmed by the owner: warm, artisanal, accessible premium; close and with character, not corporate or cold. Two interchangeable themes already exist (light / dark).

## Accessibility
Spanish (es-MX) only. Counter use implies large touch targets and fast scanning; keep WCAG AA contrast.
